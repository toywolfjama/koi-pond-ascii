# koi-pond-ascii

An animated koi pond drawn entirely with text characters on a fixed grid. Click or drag on the water to drop food: ripples spread out, pellets plop in, and the koi swim up to eat.

One self-contained `index.html`: no libraries, images or network requests. Sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser (double-click works).

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Click / tap water | Drop a handful of food (ripples + splash) |
| Drag on water | Scatter a trail of food |
| `F` / `Space` / `Enter` | Drop food at a random spot |
| `V` or `[ 3d tank ]` | Switch between the top-down pond and a side-on 3D aquarium view |
| `N` or `[ night ]` | Toggle night (fireflies, moon reflection, crickets, frogs) |
| `M` or `[ sound ]` | Mute / unmute |
| `P` | Pause |

## What's in the pond

- Ten koi varieties (kohaku, sanke, showa, utsuri, tancho, ogon, platinum, kigoi, chagoi, asagi) with swimming bodies, flapping fins, flowing tails and shadows on the pond floor; some are long-finned butterfly koi
- Koi chase food, get full, and slowly grow as they eat
- Caustic light on the water, pebbles, swaying weeds and duckweed
- Lily pads with veins and dew, lotus flowers, cattails, raked gravel and a stone border
- A cherry tree shedding petals that float on the current
- A bamboo spout that drips and knocks, and a visiting dragonfly
- A 3D aquarium view of the same koi: perspective glass tank, side-on fish with dorsal fins and tail fans, plants they swim in front of and behind, sand, rocks, driftwood, a bubbler and a snail on the glass; food drops in, floats, then sinks
